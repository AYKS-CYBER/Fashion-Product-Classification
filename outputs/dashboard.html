👔 ↔ 👕

Shirt vs. T-shirt/top Confusion

The single highest confusion in Fashion-MNIST occurs between collared shirts (Class 6) and tees (Class 0). Because images are 28x28 grayscale, collar buttons and plackets are often only 1-2 pixels wide. CNNs mitigate this by extracting high-frequency edge gradients along the neck region.

🧥 ↔ 🧶

Coat vs. Pullover Confusion

Coats (Class 4) and pullovers (Class 2) share nearly identical outer silhouettes. The distinguishing feature is the vertical center opening/zipper seam. Data augmentation with subtle zoom and horizontal flipping enables the CNN to learn zipper contrast indicators.
